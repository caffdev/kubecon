A fleet has to be run as one: clusters built, upgraded and placed from code, not one at a time, not by name. Click. The second is delivery. There is no manual way to stay on top of this. Twenty platform tools, sixty-five apps, a new Kubernetes minor three times a year, on every cluster. If a person has to touch each one, we lose, so every change rides one pipeline: in waves, behind gates, inside windows. Click. The third is agents. AI is going to solve real problems for us, and it brings new ones with it. Agents run as workloads on our nodes, act as users of our tools, and in attackers' hands they find new holes in our software every day. So we fence ours in first, and then we use them to fight back.
