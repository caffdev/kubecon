This is where we think the fleet should be heading in 2027: a north star, built from four days and five hundred talks at KubeCon in Salt Lake City. We brought back three takeaways, one per mountain. Click. One, the fleet. There's always another cluster: another environment, another exchange, another region, another cloud. So the next cluster has to be the rule, not the exception: built, upgraded and placed from code. Click. Two, delivery. No manual way keeps up. Twenty platform tools, dozens of apps, a new Kubernetes minor three times a year. If a person has to touch each one, we lose. So every change rides one pipeline. Click. Three, agents. AI brings problems as it solves them. Agents will do real work for us, and they also run on our nodes, use our tools, and in attackers' hands find new holes in what we run. So we fence ours in first, then use them to fight back. The orange trail is our team, the same small team the whole way. The star in the corner is where it's heading; it burns brighter on the slides that matter most. The first takeaway is about us. It started at four clusters.
