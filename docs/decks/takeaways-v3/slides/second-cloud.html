Hoping AWS never goes down isn’t a plan, so a second cloud joined.

For a while the plan was fingers crossed: hope AWS doesn't have a bad day. That isn't a plan. So a second cloud joins, for the day AWS does. That's normal at this scale. Nearly every large fleet at the show was hybrid or multi-cloud. Now pull back and look at the whole thing. Twenty-nine clusters. On our hardware at two exchanges, in two AWS regions, and in a second cloud. Three kinds of place, three upgrade procedures. Six reasonable steps. Every one of them made sense on the day. And there's still one person in orange. Hold on to "fingers crossed". We'll come back to it. Now look at what one upgrade costs him.
