What a developer never has to do

  • learn a cluster name
  • click sync
  • read the policy list
  • roll back by hand
So what does a developer actually have to know now? Almost nothing about the platform. Follow the route. The developer does the first two things: opens a pull request with their constraints and asks, and gets it reviewed and merged. That's their whole job. Everything after that is the platform. The change record gets approved; that's the one human sign-off the business needs, and it's projected into the cluster where policy can read it. The window opens. Karmada places the work by its constraints. It rolls out in waves through the gates we saw. And it runs. Underneath: the pack from the path decks, now empty. A developer never learns a cluster name, never clicks sync, never reads the policy list, and never rolls back by hand. The platform does all four. That's the first half solved, on paper at least.
