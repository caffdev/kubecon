The big names all run fleets: another cluster
is the rule, not the exception.

One box per talk, Nov 9–12. Lit: fleet, multi-cluster, upgrades, cluster lifecycle, by our keyword match on title and abstract.

Before our own story, here's the first takeaway as the show told it. These are the four days, every talk as a box, Monday's co-located day through Thursday. Lit are the talks about running fleets: multi-cluster, upgrades, cluster lifecycle. Almost a hundred of them. And look at who gave them. Bloomberg on multi-cluster. AWS's keynote. NVIDIA and Salesforce on one kubectl across many clusters. Apple and Uber keynotes. NVIDIA again, five thousand nodes in minutes. Mastercard, thirty EKS clusters. Coinbase, Nutanix, LinkedIn. Three to remember. LinkedIn: more than two hundred and fifty clusters, about half a million nodes, upgraded with skip-level upgrades. Salesforce: a thousand clusters, run with agents. And GEICO, a regulated insurer like the companies we compare ourselves to: no single cloud had enough GPUs, so they run Kubernetes across providers. None of them planned to have that many. Every one of those fleets grew one reasonable cluster at a time. There's always another cluster coming, so it has to be the rule, not the exception. Now ours. It started at four.
