KubeCon NA 2026, brought home

Rails, not runbooks

One team, a fleet that keeps growing, and the AI agents arriving on it.

Evan and Kyle · from four days in Salt Lake City, November 9–12

Rails, not runbooks. Behind the title, the three peaks you'll see all talk. On the left, the fleet: the clusters themselves. In the middle, delivery: how every change reaches them. On the right, agents: the newest kind of user, arriving on all of it. And along the foot of the range, the idea that ties them together. Today, most change reaches our clusters through a runbook: a person, a checklist, a terminal. What we saw at KubeCon, from teams ten times our size, is change that runs on rails: one track every change rides, a person's or an agent's, with the checks built into the track. That's the whole argument. The next forty minutes are the evidence, and the config it takes. Let's start with one number.
