A ring only moves outward when its step is healthy. Healthy means a gate, and a gate is a Job. On the left, the whole thing: a Kubernetes Job with one annotation that matters, the Argo CD PostSync hook. After Argo syncs the new Prometheus, it runs this check: are ninety-nine percent of targets up, and has that held for fifteen minutes. Every tool brings its own check. Exit zero: the app goes healthy and the next wave starts. Click. Exit one: the sync fails, and the next wave never starts. Prod never sees it. On the right, LinkedIn's slide from their canary pipeline for critical cluster services. Same shape: Argo syncs the canary, a PostSync hook decides. Click. Their result: a release went from about a week to about an hour, validated automatically, with more than ten incidents caught before production. One thing to be precise about: Argo CD doesn't roll back by itself. A failed gate stops the wave; the revert is a commit, which a bot can open. And the pipeline also needs to know when not to run at all.
