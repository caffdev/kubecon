Pause on this one, because it's the slide the platform half exists to deliver. Today, Argo CD is a dashboard somebody clicks. And teams don't resist auto-sync because they like clicking. They resist it because manual sync is the only change control they have. Take the button away and you've taken away the one thing standing between a bad commit and production. Of course they say no. So the offer isn't "turn on auto-sync". The offer is a trade. You stop clicking sync. Click. In exchange, nothing ships during a freeze, because the window is in the AppProject, not in someone's head. Click. A bad version stops at the canary, because the gate Job fails and the next wave never starts. Click. And a policy violation fails in CI in ninety seconds, not at two in the morning. That last one is coming, and it turns out to be the same rule we'll hand the agents. Notice the order. The control comes back in a better form before the click goes away. Prod auto-sync is the last thing we switch on, not the first thing we ask for. That's the platform team. Now developers, and where their app runs.
