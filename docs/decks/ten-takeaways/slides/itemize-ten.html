Rule ten: itemize it, or it didn't happen. Wait for the receipt to print. On it, what we saw other teams itemise. Verkada knows its alert triage costs thirty-three cents an alert, on about forty-seven hundred alerts a month; the fifteen hundred dollars is our arithmetic on their numbers. Nubank traces sixteen billion spans an hour. Yahoo tamed Istio's metric cardinality at ten thousand pods. Adobe, not on the receipt, attributes network cost back to teams with eBPF. Then our tab. Can we list what we use AI for? Not yet. Spend by team? Not yet. Every MCP call as a span? Not yet. Total known: nothing. We can't pick the right model per use case, or defend the spend, until every call is a line item. The fix is boring and it comes first: turn on telemetry, and put every call on the OpenTelemetry pipeline we already run, with a team and a use case on it. The inventory falls out of the instrumentation. Ten rules. Three of them we can start on Monday.
