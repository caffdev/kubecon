We came home with ten rules, and none of them is a product.

KubeCon NA 2026, Salt Lake City. Evan and Kyle.

We spent four days in Salt Lake City and came home with ten things. Here's the promise: every one of them is a rule we can adopt, not a tool we have to buy. They come in the order you'd build them. Pause on the range. The first three are the ground the fleet stands on. The next two are the path every change takes across it. The last five are what agents need before we let them in. Ten rules in ten slides means two slides carry a pair. I'll say when. The first rule is about the thing that gets big whatever we do.
