KARGO OR GITOPS PROMOTER?

HELD: 2-WEEK SPIKE

Rule four: nothing leaves the station without a signal. Promotion is literally a train leaving one station for the next: dev, stage, prod. And a departure board is a public, timestamped record of what left and when, which is exactly what we want from GitOps. Read the board. LinkedIn: canary namespaces with an Argo CD gate, a week per release down to an hour. Meta: Terraform on a cron job replaced by Argo CD and Kargo, a day down to minutes. Transfix: agents earn the right to merge, from shadow mode to a granted permission. Ford: CI as the safety net under AI-written GitOps, across hundreds of clusters. And our row, in amber: Kargo or GitOps Promoter, held for a two-week spike. The signal is the pipeline, not a person reading every diff. And the signal is decided by a rule that doesn't care who wrote the change.
