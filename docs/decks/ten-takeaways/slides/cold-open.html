KubeCon NA 2026, Salt Lake City. Evan and Kyle.

Ten things we brought home from Salt Lake City, about a minute each. Here's the promise: every one of them is a rule we can adopt, not a tool we have to buy. They come in the order you'd build them. The first three are the ground the fleet stands on. The next two are the path every change takes across it. The last five are what agents need before we let them in. The colours are the same three paths we use everywhere else. The first rule is about the thing that's going to get big whatever we do.
