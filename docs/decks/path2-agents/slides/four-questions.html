Four keynotes this week, Agent Substrate, Sandbox Your Agents, The Harness Is the New Runtime, and The Identity Crisis, all named the same checklist. For any agent we run, we have to be able to say where four things come from. Its sandbox: the code it wrote a minute ago runs somewhere, and a container shares a kernel. Its identity: who it is, and for how long, ideally minutes rather than forever. Its egress: what it can reach, and through what. And its session: where its state lives, and what happens if it dies halfway through a task. Right now, for Claude on a laptop, the honest answer to all four is "the laptop, with one person's credentials". That's why the walls are drawn open. If we can't answer all four, it isn't production-ready. Three of the four are much cheaper to build in than to retrofit. We could build all four into a new platform. We think that's the mistake.
