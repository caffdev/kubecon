The teams we heard from with good accuracy numbers all budgeted two things that nobody puts in an AI business case by default. On the left, a curated knowledge base. Runbooks and design docs, kept current on purpose, with chat threads weighted down. Salesforce credited this, not a model change, for their accuracy across a thousand clusters. STCLab found that runbook quality mattered more than which model they used. Write the runbook the agent follows, and it gets better as the runbook does. On the right, an evaluation harness. A rubric, graded by a model, plus a stratified sample checked by people. Built before launch, so we can tell a better prompt from a lucky week. Both are unglamorous, both need an owner, and both are what separated the teams with good numbers from everyone else. They're the two owners we'll ask for at the end. All of this starts on laptops, this month.
