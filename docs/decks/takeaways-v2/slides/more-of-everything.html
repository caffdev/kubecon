Every new team brought apps, users and, usually, another cluster.

This is the step nobody decides. Each new team arrives with apps, with people who deploy them, and usually with a reason to want a cluster of its own: isolation, a compliance boundary, a region. Every one of those requests is reasonable on the day. Fourteen clusters. About sixty apps. Thirty-odd people deploying. Notice the cluster names have gone from the map. There are too many to name now, and nobody misses them. And he's still one person. Then some of the users stopped being people.
