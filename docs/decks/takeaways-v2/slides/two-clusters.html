We started with two clusters, test and prod, and one person who upgrades them.

Let's start small, because we did. A team, a handful of people, and two clusters on our own hardware. One to test on, one to run on. We built them with kubeadm, by hand, and that was the right call at the time. And there's one person (that's him, in orange, with the runbook) who knows how to upgrade them. Three times a year, Kubernetes ships a new minor version, and he walks both clusters through it. It takes him a couple of days. Nobody else notices. Keep an eye on him. He's in every picture from here on. Then prod became important enough to need a twin.
