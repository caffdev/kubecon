Policy stops what shouldn't happen. Telemetry shows what did, to whoever is debugging, person or agent. The trick is one collector config on every cluster. The k8sattributes processor stamps every signal with its namespace, pod and deployment; the highlighted part copies the team label from the namespace. The resource processor stamps the cluster. Prometheus stays; this sits in front of it. Click. One incident, four signals: the alert, the failing span, the error log, the Kubernetes event. Same cluster, same team, and the span and log share a trace id. A person can pivot between them. An agent can't do it any other way. The receipt is cost. Yahoo cut Istio metrics from two million series to two hundred and eighty thousand with two-stage aggregation: about a hundred and forty thousand dollars a year on one cluster.
