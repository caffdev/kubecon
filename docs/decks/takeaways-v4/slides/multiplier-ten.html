Illustrative model, not our measurements: four releases a year per tool, 1.5 h per install by hand (assets/toil-model.json). Real numbers replace it before this goes beyond the team.

Ours started at four clusters: dev, staging, and production at two exchanges. Those are the four columns on the left. Each row is one of the platform tools that runs on them, Argo CD, Prometheus, the Splunk collector and so on, and each lit cell holds four ticks: four releases a year that somebody rolls out by hand. Now watch it grow. Every step was reasonable. Click: EKS for dev, qa and ftb. Click: the second exchange. Click: the migration, six clusters in AWS. Click: a second region, because fingers crossed for AWS isn't a plan. Click: a second cloud. Twenty-nine clusters. Twenty tools: most of them everywhere, Ceph and Rook only on our hardware, Karpenter and external-dns only in the cloud, a few on three clusters. That's four hundred and eighteen installs, four releases each: sixteen hundred and seventy-two rollouts a year, by hand. About twenty-five hundred hours. It's a model, not a measurement, and the team behind it is the same small team. And that's before a single cluster upgrade, or the apps. There's always another cluster.
