is a dozen lines of ApplicationSet.

Progressive sync sits behind an ApplicationSet controller flag: check its maturity in our Argo CD before we lean on it.

Twenty tools across the fleet. The pipeline that rolls them out is less than you'd think. On the right, the order: test cluster, one canary, then prod a quarter at a time. Each ring is a label on the cluster: wave equals test, canary or prod. On the left, the whole rollout order for Prometheus: an ApplicationSet over every registered cluster. The line that matters is RollingSync. Three steps: test; when healthy, canary; when healthy, prod, no more than twenty-five percent at a time. The template copies each cluster's label onto its app, so the steps can find it. Same file shape for all twenty tools. A thirtieth cluster arrives labelled, from the Region claim, and joins its ring. The catch: progressive sync sits behind a controller flag. We check its maturity in our Argo CD before we lean on it. A ring only moves outward when its step is healthy. Healthy means a gate.
