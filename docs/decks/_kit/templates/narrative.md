## Brief

- **Audience:** who is in the room, what they already know, what they care about
- **Setting:** live talk / sent to read / both, and how long
- **They believe now:** …
- **They believe after:** …
- **The one sentence:** the idea someone could repeat in the hallway afterwards
- **The ask:** the specific thing you want them to do

## Shape

- **Structure:** which arc, and why it fits this audience
- **Acts:** I … / II … / III …
- **The peak:** which slide is the turn, and why it sits there
- **The close:** how the ending answers the opening

## Visual direction

Written in phase 3, before theme.css: palette (named hexes), type, layout idea, the one
signature element, how concepts become pictures, the one motion moment, and what you changed
after checking the plan against the generic defaults.

## Beats

One `###` heading per slide, named with the slide's slug. `deck sync` creates the files;
the overview shows these fields beside each slide; Energy (1–5) draws the pacing curve.

### cold-open

- **Act:** I
- **Job:** what this slide has to make the audience feel or know
- **Headline:** the assertion on screen (or the spoken line, if the slide is wordless)
- **Visual:** the concept made visible: what it looks like, not what it says
- **Energy:** 3
- **Into next:** the line that pulls us to the next slide
