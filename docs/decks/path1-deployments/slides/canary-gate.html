If you remember one mechanism from this deck, make it this one. A Rollout is the workload's spec, in Git. It sends a share of traffic to the new version and pauses. An AnalysisRun queries a metric, error rate or latency, and judges it against a threshold we wrote down in advance. Click. When the analysis fails, the version reverts itself. Nobody is paged to make the call, nobody has to notice the graph. That automatic fail-back is the whole ask on this slide.
