Here's the trade made concrete, starting with stage A, by January: Argo becomes a control loop, in non-prod. Six items. Auto-sync with self-heal and prune, non-prod only; prod stays manual until stage B. The guardrails ship in the same change, which is the rule from earlier. Stakater Reloader, which gets its own slide next because it's the trust prerequisite. A drift policy by resource class: silent self-heal for cluster-critical things, alert first where a human may have hot-fixed something. An Argo CD upgrade. And a reclaim-policy audit on our storage classes. The exit gate is how we know the stage is done rather than declared done: zero manual syncs in non-prod for four weeks, drift alerts going to a named owner, and a config change that demonstrably rolls the pods that use it. And the first two weeks are deliberately small: one app, and the real deliverable is the list of everything that drifted. Nobody argues with a list of things that were already wrong. One of those six items deserves its own slide.
