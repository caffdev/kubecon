Five decisions, and every one already has a lean, so this is about confirming, not deliberating. Take the objections, but let's not reopen promotion: the spike is how we test it. Prod auto-sync comes after the change-window gating, because the gating is what buys the permission. Promoter first, Kargo as the fallback, and a two-week spike on one app. Change records projected into the cluster as a CRD, because admission can't depend on an outside service. Canary as a proof of concept per workload class, where "not yet" is an allowed answer. And drift handled both ways, split by resource class: silent self-heal for cluster-critical things, alert first where someone may have hot-fixed. One smaller question we'd like an answer to: does any app's config genuinely need in-place refresh? We lean no, and if none does, the config-server refresh path retires. The card on the right is the thing to repeat at the end. Stage B has no demo and no dashboard. A room that buys the trade and then funds only stage A has made things worse, because auto-sync without the policy layer is exactly the risk the guardrails exist for. It also gates the agent path's pull requests. If we leave with one owner named, make it stage B's.
