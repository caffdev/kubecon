Here's where we are, and let's say it without being defensive. Argo CD is installed in every cluster, on manual sync. That's stage zero, and it's honest. But look at what that one button is standing in for. Drift is something we find by accident, and a config change can report success while the pods keep serving the old value. Policy is a reviewer's memory; we have no engine. The freeze is someone refusing to click. Promotion is a person editing YAML in another repo. And rollback is a person and a Slack thread. So the button isn't really a deploy button. It's the only change control we've got. That's why teams hold on to it, and they're right to. The letter beside each one is the stage that replaces it. Hold on to those letters; they come back. Here's what replaces it, starting with the one that matters most: rollback.
