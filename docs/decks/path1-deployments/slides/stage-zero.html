Here's where we are, and let's say it without being defensive. Argo CD is installed in every cluster, on manual sync. That's stage zero, and it's honest. But look at what that one button is standing in for. Promotion is a person editing YAML in another repo. Policy is a reviewer's memory; we have no policy engine. Rollback is a person and a Slack thread. Drift is something we discover by accident. And a config change can report success while the pods keep serving the old value. A dashboard somebody clicks isn't a deployment platform. It's a deployment interface. Nothing downstream, promotion, canary, agents, has anything to drive until this changes. Before we ask to turn that button off, one rule.
