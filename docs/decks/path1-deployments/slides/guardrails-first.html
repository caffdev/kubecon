Before we ask anyone to turn off the sync button, one rule, and it's a practice we're adopting, not a scare story. Auto-sync ships with its guardrails in the same pull request. Not a follow-up ticket. One change. The first line is the feature: auto-sync with self-heal, in non-prod only. Click. Prune can never reach stateful or cluster-critical resources. Click. Deletions go last, after everything else is healthy. Click. Prod keeps a sync window, and stays manual until stage B. Click. And we audit storage reclaim policy, because Delete is the default and it's the wrong default for data we care about. The teams at the show who run GitOps at scale talked about these as a named practice with a checklist. It's what makes the guardrails look like part of the feature rather than overhead.
