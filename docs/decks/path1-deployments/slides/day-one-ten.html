Path 1: Deployments as a service
Evan and Kyle

This is CVS Health, on the main stage on Wednesday. Read it with me: approachable enough that someone joining the company could ship a new application on their very first day. Our first reaction was probably the same as yours. We are never letting someone deploy to prod on day one. Day one, they can't find the bathroom. Get the laugh. Then: that's not what they meant. Day one doesn't measure how much we trust new hires. It measures how much of our platform a person has to understand before they can ship. CVS didn't make production safer by trusting people more. They made the path so constrained that trust stopped being the variable. That peak on the right is where this deck is going. There's no route on it yet. And our peers have already climbed it.
