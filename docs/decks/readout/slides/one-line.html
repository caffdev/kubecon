We heard it five different ways, in five different tracks, from people who had already shipped it. Let agents propose. Let progressive delivery and policy decide. Pause. Here's why that line answers both forces. On the left, the fleet: more clusters than there are hands to upgrade them. On the right, agents: a new kind of workload that needs sandboxing and identity, and a new kind of user that wants to change our infrastructure. Follow both routes up and they arrive at the same col. A change has to reach a cluster safely without a person reading every line, whether there are too many clusters for people to keep up, or the author isn't a person at all. The fix for both is the same: GitOps, policy and progressive delivery. Nobody this week argued for a human reading every diff. And nobody argued for an agent with production credentials and no gate. So where are we standing when we start, and what ties the climbs together?
