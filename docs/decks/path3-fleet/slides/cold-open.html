Forty squares. Each one is a business day. That's how long it used to take LinkedIn to move production across three Kubernetes minor versions, 1.31 to 1.34: six fleet waves, one minor at a time. This year it took seven. Same fleet, one rollout. The newer control-plane binaries emulate the older minor's behaviour, which is KEP-4330, so three minors become one wave instead of six.
