We opened with forty days becoming seven. The ask is to make that our policy rather than someone else's exception. Five decisions. E1: EKS is our container orchestrator, and new ECS workloads need a written exception. E2: new clusters default to Auto Mode, with self-managed Karpenter only where it doesn't fit. E3: no cluster ever enters extended support, and every cluster has a named owner. Then the cluster-label schema needs an owner today, because it's stage 1 and three things wait on it. And decision 2: the list of production workloads that can't run active-active, named and counted. If that list is empty, Karmada stays on the watch list and the decision closes. And one thing we're starting unless someone objects: a skip-level upgrade pilot in non-prod this quarter, paired with a timed restore drill, so we're willing to upgrade aggressively.
