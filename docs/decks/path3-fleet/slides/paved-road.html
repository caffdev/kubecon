Above the seam, developers still have no road. We have no internal developer platform today, so every team invents its own way to deploy. Here are the options, laid out by how much of Kubernetes they hide. A portal like Backstage, Port or Cortex: a catalog, golden-path templates and scorecards; Backstage was a full day of the show, and it's a TypeScript application we'd own and staff, not a weekend install. A workload spec like Score or OpenChoreo: one portable file per workload. An orchestrator like Humanitec or Kratix: declare what a workload needs and the platform wires it. And at the far end, a PaaS: push code, get an app; Salesforce runs on Heroku. Our lean is the far left: a paved road with no portal yet. Templates in Git, ApplicationSets, the cluster-label vocabulary, and policy-enforced defaults. As one talk put it, you probably already have one. It's useful on day one, and it keeps every portal option open. Every company that made deploying boring standardised the path first. The portal came afterwards, as a window onto it.
