Last, the floor under all of it, and the one area where being wrong is unrecoverable. Backups are a rumor until we restore one. Three steps, in order. Retain: audit every storage class, because Delete is the default reclaim policy and it's the wrong one for data we care about. Back up: Velero on a schedule, with consistent, incremental snapshots, and nothing bespoke, because there's now a Kubernetes data protection working group standardising exactly this. Drill: restore into a scratch cluster on a calendar, and time it. The drill matters more than the backup. A cluster we can rebuild and restore is a cluster we're willing to upgrade aggressively, which is the whole deck. And one cheap alert: etcd database size at fifty and seventy-five percent. AWS showed how pod status churn alone can push a cluster read-only with zero crashed nodes. Catch it in days, not minutes. So, five decisions.
