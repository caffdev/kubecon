Here's the one sentence for the whole talk. We are early enough to build, from the first EKS cluster, what the big operators had to retrofit. This curve is the shape of the next four years if we grow the way we plan to. We are here, at the bottom left, before the bend. Every company you'll hear about today hit the bend first and fixed their platform afterwards, while it was running. That's expensive. We get to do it the other way round: decide how a cluster, a service and an agent are made before there are a hundred of them. First, what we're leaving on the floor in Salt Lake City.
