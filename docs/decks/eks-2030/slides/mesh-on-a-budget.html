Sidecar or ambient: decide before the first prod clusterCoinbase is moving to ambient.

Istio gives us identity. At scale, it also gives us metrics: lots of them, because every pair of services talking makes its own series. It's an n-squared problem. Yahoo measured one production cluster: twenty-four thousand pods in the mesh, four hundred thousand requests a second, and two point oh six million active Istio metric series. That's the long bar. They aggregated in two stages inside the cluster, per node and then across the cluster, before anything left for the backend. Two hundred and eighty thousand series. That's the short bar, drawn to the same scale. Eighty-six percent fewer, and about a hundred and forty thousand dollars a year back, on that one cluster. Click. Two more rules from the show. Rapido, at ten thousand pods, cut config churn by scoping what each proxy has to track to its namespace. And GEICO runs Cilium default-deny underneath Istio, for the things a mesh can't see: UDP, traffic that bypasses the sidecar, and system pods. Click. And one decision we owe ourselves before the first production cluster: sidecar or ambient. Coinbase is moving to ambient. That cardinality lesson is the whole observability story in miniature.
