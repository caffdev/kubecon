Here's 2030, written as a spec sheet. These are our proposed targets, not facts, and each one sits next to a company that already does it today. A new EKS cluster: same day, from a pull request, no tickets. Meta does that with about fifteen people. A Kubernetes minor version across the whole fleet in under two weeks. LinkedIn does it in seven business days across two hundred and fifty clusters. A new service is about ten lines from the app team. Zero long-lived secrets in pods. Every alert gets a first look from an agent in minutes, for cents. Routine change is opened by agents and approved by a person; that row is red because a person stays in it. Every dollar has an owner. And the footprint: at least two AWS regions, and on-prem exited. That last one has a cloud around it because we haven't decided it. Fully exit, or keep on-prem as one more cluster run exactly like the others? Five moves get us there. Each one is already running at a company that spoke last week.
