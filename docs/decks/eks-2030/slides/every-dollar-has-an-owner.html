If we grow exponentially, so does the bill. So every dollar needs an owner from the first cluster, not after the first finance review. Adobe drew the problem at the show. The AWS bill comes per account. Ownership is per team. Teams spread across clusters and accounts, and by default nothing lines up. Click. Four meters. Pods: a team label on everything, enforced at admission, so it's never optional. Packets: Adobe priced network flows with eBPF, because cross-AZ, peering and internet egress all cost differently. Series: Yahoo's hundred and forty thousand a year was one cluster's metrics. And tokens: Verkada's triage agent costs about twenty-seven cents for a five-call investigation and a dollar twenty-nine past twenty-five calls. Mastercard said the same thing: once agents run off a laptop, they need budgets and attribution. Tokens bring us to move five: the agents.
