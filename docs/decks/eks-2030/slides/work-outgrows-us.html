LinkedIn and Mastercard: their posted slides. Curves are illustrative, not data.

This is the problem in one picture. The black line is the work: every cluster times every release times every CVE. The red line is the team. Teams grow in a straight line, one hire at a time. The work doesn't. The blue gap between them is toil. Someone does it by hand, or it doesn't get done. LinkedIn put a formula on it. Upgrade time is rollouts times ramps times deploy-plus-soak, divided by your daily window. For three minor versions across two hundred and fifty clusters, done the normal way, that came to forty business days. And this is what we wrote down ourselves on the show floor: lots of companies are running hundreds of clusters, and we can't keep doing manual upgrades. Click. Then AI multiplies it. Mastercard's speaker had a nice pair: every CTO says AI means ten times the productivity; every CISO says it means ten times the vulnerabilities. Both are right, and both land on this curve. Every big operator at the show hit this wall. The ones who hit it late paid for it.
