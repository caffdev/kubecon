Here's the road, one rung a year. Each rung has a test we can fail, so we'll know whether we're on it. Click. 2027 is the foundation. The first production EKS clusters are built from Git, and apps arrive on the ten-line contract. Vault and Istio identity from day one, and the OTel tier in front of Prometheus, Grafana and Splunk. And our first agent: alert triage, read-only. The test: can we rebuild a production cluster from Git in a day? Click. 2028 is the move. Most apps are on EKS and on-prem is draining. A second AWS region. Upgrades go in waves with checks, and agents start opening the upgrade and CVE merge requests. The test: one Kubernetes minor version across the whole fleet in two weeks. Click. 2029 is scale. Tens of clusters. Agents ship routine change with one human approval. Cost per team, per agent and per alert is on a dashboard. The test is the one that matters: the platform team stays flat while the cluster count doubles. Click. And 2030 is the ratio: every line of the spec sheet, met. The test: our row belongs next to Meta's. What we don't build matters just as much.
