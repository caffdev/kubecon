Move four: one telemetry pipeline. We keep Prometheus, Grafana and Splunk. What we add is one OpenTelemetry tier in front of them. Read it left to right. Thousands of services, but they're instrumented once, in a handful of shared libraries: HTTP, Kafka, the database clients. Everything goes through an OTel agent on each node, then a gateway that aggregates and samples. Then out to the backends we already pay for: metrics to Prometheus and Grafana, logs and traces to Splunk. Changing a backend becomes exporter config. Nobody redeploys a service. Click. Nubank runs this with five engineers at about twenty-three billion spans an hour. Their trick was instrumenting six libraries instead of four thousand services. Click. Splunk co-presented the OTel Blueprints talk: one prescribed collector shape for every team. remberg ships Grafana dashboards as pull requests. And Best Buy's advice for incident response: page on SLO burn, not CPU, and put deploys on the timeline. That's the context our agents will need later, too. Once every signal goes through one place, so can every dollar.
